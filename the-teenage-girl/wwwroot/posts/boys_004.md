A bit of a backstory into this guy I potentially had a crush on for honestly too long so you can make your own idea of it...

While going through my things and packing for my move, I came across my journal that I hadn’t written into in forever. 
Of course, the usual ritual of reading through the entirety of it ensued and I struck gold. Mind you this is about a boy 
from high school but I though it was so good I had to share, so here it goes, listen to the rambling of 18 year old me:

**June 1st, 2024**

“It really is true what they say about words versus actions because why would you bring up the fact that I liked you and 
talk about it for 45 minutes just the two of us in a kitchen and then, on other occasions, run away at the first phrase 
that could be interpreted as me liking you? This when I also expressly asked you not to because it really hurts me. And 
more it seems to only be with certain people because the other day, while in a room with only people who knew about it, 
the only person who didn’t kinda asked about it and everyone stayed silent but you encouraged him? Why? What did you 
gain from this? Why when someone says something you run away but yet you feel at liberty to make jokes yourself?

Of course you don’t care, after all it’s not you who gets hurt in either scenario. It’s only me. It’s always only me. 
Alone and unrequited. So you should understand why sometimes I can get a little salty with you.

I just keep thinking you’re going to change for some reason. But of course you never fail to disappoint. I don’t know 
what’s going on in your head, I don’t know what’s up with you. If that kitchen talk was just drunk banter. Heck I don’t 
even know if I like you or if you’re just some messed up case I’m trying to solve.

Sometimes I even wonder if we really are friends. I mean you hardly ever open up to me. Last time you somewhat shared 
something about you was months ago and that was before people knew, when I was still undercover. I wish I could just drop 
everything and kiss you but that feels as hard as jumping from a bridge when you’re not suicidal. You just make it so hard. 
Are you running away from me or something more? Are you running away from yourself? Am I making all of this up in my head? 
Do you even give two shits about me? Will you ever really know what I feel about you? Will I? And will I ever have the 
guts to tell you?

You’re so far yet so close. It’s like every time I try to reach you, you go away. Just like that one Greek myth. Oh well, 
maybe you’re just a bit too Greek for me. Too mythical. Heck you’re not even my type. What do I even find in you? Okay 
you’re ridiculously hot but then? Smart? yes. Funny? well you know what I think about that.

And that’s why I’m not actually in love with you. I think I just want you to see me as a person and actually respect me, 
which I don’t feel like when you run away from me. And I also really do want to kiss you that’s true. Maybe even more, 
that’s possible, who knows. But you’re just too strange. And I also know that if I don’t try something before I leave 
I’ll regret it forever. And I don’t think I’ll regret trying and fucking it up nearly as much.”

So now for a bit of context and back story. I’ve known this guy for 15 years now, 13 years at that point. I always kinda 
liked him cause he was cute but for some reason I also always felt like I was just convincing myself that I liked him. 
Like yes, I’d get really giddy when he would text me (as friends of course) but I don’t know, I never wanted to make a 
move or anything (even though I’m always the one making the move with every guy ever. SO for 2 years I was kind of in 
this constant state of questioning myself as to whether I really liked him or not and it was worse than just not liking 
him at all.

Now for added context of that night in the kitchen: turns out he was actually talking to this girl that I knew for just 
as long (the prettiest girl in school of course), but she had rejected him because she was religious. All this while also 
actively talking to another girl, one of our shared friend’s little sister, and asking her out on a date the weekend 
before and staying over. This guy and 3 of my other friends were going to Greece together that summer ,so about a month 
after that night happened and while in the kitchen, while confronting me about MY feelings, he asked me why I’d never 
told him to which I answered “We both know why”. And he then proceeded to say “Well what happens in Greece stays in 
Greece”, after he’d told one of my friends who knew I liked him that i was “nice” and “anything could happen”.

Basically that's the gist of it, I’ll let you make your own idea of this whole thing.
