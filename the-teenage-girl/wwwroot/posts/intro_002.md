I am moving to uni in **less than 2 weeks**.

That would sound pretty normal for a girl my age except I’m not only moving into a dorm, I’m also moving on another 
continent, away from family and friends, where they speak another language and in a country where I know absolutely no 
one. (Yes I have an aunt in Pennsylvania but that is still another country and several hours away by plane.)

Now don’t get me wrong I’m very excited to move there, I mean I’ve wanted it for years now, ever since 8th grade 
actually, but still I can’t wrap my head around the fact that’s it actually happening. I have been planning this for so 
long, from the college applications to 8 different schools to the intense cramming to get the scores required by my 
conditional admission (I crushed it by the way).

Up until a week ago, when my visa finally got approved I just convinced myself I wasn’t gonna go, and I was just gonna 
study at Paris Cité to not get my heart crushed when the obvious “no” from the IRCC was going to drop in my mailbox. 
Except the email didn’t say no. And since then I have been even more stressed than the two months during which I was 
waiting for the answer.

I had already had a bit of an adrenaline rush, mixed with fear, sadness and apprehension when my mom had sent me the 
booking of the plane tickets to go to Canada. I had to try really hard and not cry right in the middle of the beach in 
Greece surrounded by my friends, but I managed to keep a straight face and sweep the fear under the rug.

Only now it’s all very real, and I’m gonna have to pack my bags in like 2 days and I have a week to say goodbye to all 
my friends, some of which I've known for 13 years.

So during the past month I did what any girl in my situation would do and I thought about what I wanted for my future. 
If everything goes according to plan, I’m supposed to graduate in five years and after that potentially move back to 
France. Except I’m not really sure I’ll want to go back to France after all that.

Actually if I really listen to what I want right now (and I know it’s only right now but bear with me), I want to try 
and get an internship at Vogue during one of my coop sequences so that I can maybe get offered a job after I graduate, 
and then I’ll be like the girl in a romcom and I’ll work in a fashion magazine (although in the IT department and not 
as a journalist but still) and then I’ll have dinner parties with my girlfriends and a beautiful apartment in a high 
rise, all this while living in New York of course.

And then a few years down the line I’ll move to London, maybe start my own company I don’t know yet, and I’ll meet a 
guy, and I’ll think “Oh I wish I’d met you sooner” and we’ll love each other for a while and have a great time. And 
when I’m even older I’ll buy an old house in Italy in a little convivial village, maybe with him, maybe not, and I’ll 
finish my life in the Italian countryside.

I recently discovered that I’ve always moved quite a lot. Every time I’d start making my marks somewhere, I’d move. in 
primary school after the divorce, after my first year of high school and now for uni. It’s just the way it is, and it’s 
why I don’t see myself living in just one place in my future.

And although I am in love with love, I wouldn’t chase it to the point of not allowing myself to make my own life choices 
and have the life I want and deserve. That’s why I don’t know if I’ll have kids, I don’t know if I’ll marry, but I want 
to live in places that fulfill me.
