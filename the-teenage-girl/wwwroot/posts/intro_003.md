This growing up thing is for real a pain in my a**

I have somewhat started losing my French and I have heavy English struggles from time to time and when that happens I 
fold into a kind of linguistic limbo and I heavy dissociate.

On another note, there's this other French guy here that I met on my first day picking up my keys and I saw him again 
today, and he said I really shed my accent since he saw me the first week Yipee!!!!

At the same time I'm kinda scared because language is like 80% of my personality, I mean I talk ALL THE TIME and when 
I'm not talking I'm thinking about talking and I have to actively stop myself from cutting people off or monopolizing 
the conversation. Then about 30% of the time I'm sleeping and when I do I'm monopolizing the conversation with the dream 
people.

I always fear I'm insufferable and people are bored by me and I guess maybe oversharing is my way of compensating for 
that fear, but then I get back to my room and I think about what I said and I regret it all. I also have to learn how 
to be alone again because being in uni you're basically surrounded by people all the time. Even when I go back to my 
room I hear mmy neighbours, so I'm basically never alone (I have the noisiest neighbours ever, but honestly it's fun) 
(except when they're doing karaoke in the hallway at 3 in the morning).

Anyways all this to say that, although I have gotten better at it, I still can't spend the whole day without seeing 
someone I know and feel satisfied and fulfilled with my day. Hopefully, I'll have my fix with all the people I have to 
see when I go back to France and undoubtedly get my French superpowers back.

I do very much feel like I'm growing up when the weekend comes around, and it gets to be time for me to do laundry, 
vacuum and buy little dirnks for my little fridge. To be honest I do still have a hard time doing dishes but that's 
just because I've always really hated it. I sometimes find myself fantasizing about living in my own FULL apartment 
like with my own kitchen, bathroom and the rest of the package. I also really want a job to be honest, but I also 
really wish I didn't have to go to any interviews or submit cover letters.

Stress of the finals is also doing quite the number on me, I actually found myself scrolling on facebook marketplace 
for apartments for the summer time because as of right now I am homeless as far as the summer term goes even though I 
will very much be there for 4 months and I would very much like having a ceiling over my head and walls around me.
