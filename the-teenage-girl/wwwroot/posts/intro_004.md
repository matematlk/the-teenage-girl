Having a look at things I liked this month...

For about 3 years now I have kept this note in my phone of things I like. I never delete anything from it I only ever 
add, I like to go back and see who I was. It’s like my own little personal time machine of preferences. Today, I am 
making that personal file a little less personal by sharing with you what I added in there during the month of January.

I can’t believe we’re in 2025 I don’t know what it is about this number but it feels unreal, like we’re not actually 
alive right now and we’re taking a break from the usual timeline. But one might argue I’m just crazy.

Anyways let’s get to business. First on the list is crying. I LOVE crying it’s so freeing and it gives you that little 
exhaustion that makes you fulfilled if you know what I mean. I’ve always been a big crier but lately I have really grown 
to like watching sad movies specifically to cry. I actually like crying so much that I’m happy when I get my period 
because It means I’m gonna have intense crying sessions. I just love feeling and I already feel things very deeply but 
when hormones get in there, oh boy oh boy.

Second is chewy food I am addicted to all things chewy. Mochi, tteokbokki, gum anything. That also extends to stress 
toys, I go crazy for a needoh omg the things I would do. The only thing with chewy food is that they have to be only 
chewy, they cannot have any other texture mixed in there. Like if I’m eating chicken and I bite down on cartilage 
EWWWWWWWWWW I simply cannot eat anymore and I get chills in my whole body.

Recently, I rediscovered the Internet Archive and I had a 3-hour deep dive that started with looking at gq magazines, 
and then I stumbled on the face which is a british magazine, and they had a Blur edition so of course I had to read it. 
But as I was looking at those magazines I somehow ended up on the music side of the Archive, listening to random 
mixtapes made by bands in their garage in the 2000s and cross-referencing which ones were on spotify. I ended with 30+ 
songs added to my playlist that all have 50 listens tops. (I absolutely should have been doing maths then)

To stay on the topic of music, my current listening habits have been almost exclusively alt-rock and what spotify 
describes as “post grunge” which encompasses such bands as nirvana, the smashing pumpkins and weezer. I LOVE WEEZER soso 
much like I couldn’t even say how much. More specifically I have been listening to Pinkerton on repeat this whole album 
is an absolute banger. Special mentions go out to Falling For You and Across The Sea like that second one is literally 
a description of me, but anyways I diverge.

Finally, I’ve been really loving growing up lately like I feel like I’m embracing every single aspect of it every 
hardship and every happy moment. Part of that growing up has also been embracing the cringe im bringing my inner alpha 
out now and roaring with my whole lungs… My addiction to instagram reels is growing stronger, and I am fully succumbing 
but those be hitting fr fr. I’m also evolving a way I don’t like as much, that is I don’t think I enjoy math nearly as 
much anymore. Maybe It’s just cause it has become harder, and I’m having a lot more trouble finishing even one exercise. 
To be honest CS has also been getting harder, and I’ve also been liking it a little less.

To wrap things up I want to tell you all to really enjoy every moment even if it feels stupid at first and try your 
hardest in everything you do.

Aight so long guys.
