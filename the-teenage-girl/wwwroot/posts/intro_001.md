For as long as I can remember my guy friends have been going out anytime and haven't been expected home until the sun 
was down. And even now when I tell them I get the Cinderella treatment with my mom they get so shocked. Believe me guys 
I feel you, and it's not easy every day, especially with my extreme case of FOMO.

I mean I might be biased here, but I feel like not allowing girls to go out as much as guys or simply treating them 
different just because they're girls is exactly the root of the problem. Stop me if I'm wrong but that's basically the 
definition of discrimination.

And maybe I'd get it if I was going out in frilly dresses with all my girlfriends giggling and not looking both ways 
before we cross the street, but I literally go out with a group of four guys, one o which boxes and another who does 
rugby. I'm pretty sure I'm quite protected enough. Hell, they wish I'd get attacked so they can actually fight a random 
guy in the street.

What I'm trying to say is I'm perfectly capable of surviving outside past midnight **especially** if I'm with Y chromosome 
owning friends. I mean I survive just fine when I'm at my dads and go out just about anytime. And at my age am I not 
supposed to be exploring the world and experiencing its wonders? I mean it's what teenage years are for is it not?
