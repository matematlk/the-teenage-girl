I don't know what it is about highways, but I turn into a philosopher, or maybe that's just what being a teenage girl 
is like.

I don't know if any of you are interested in what I have to say but since you clicked on this post I'm going to assume 
that you are. So get ready because here are my naked thoughts. First, let me set the scene: I am on a go bus on line 25, 
going to Square One, Mississauga, and I am all the way at the front of the bus on the second floor next to a stranger 
who I suspect has been cropdusting me since the beginning of the ride (I think the fumes actually made me think even 
harder) and I am looking at the road and the trees go by (the road is blackish dark gray btw).

All of a sudden the road turns a light gray that is almost white, and it becomes really smooth, and it makes me think of 
a mall floor you know the ones that have weird patterns so you can't tell exactly how dirty they are. And I start 
imagining I'm in a mall and this weird feeling starts growing in me and I realize I don't actually like malls, like I 
like the idea of hanging out with friends in a mall but the place itself feels like an overbearing parent, the type that 
keep you from achieving your dreams by stepping on them. Well malls have that same vibe of trying to format you and make 
you a basic bitch to sort of bring you back to zero and then make whatever they want out of you. For parents that means 
what they themselves could never be and for malls it means a little paypig, slave of capitalism ready to go broke before 
they come back out.

Also, as I was looking at endless empty-looking fields go by, I realised Ontario is like 80% unused space. Like can you 
imagine the amount of walmarts and super parking lots they could build there? All that space is just untapped ressources 
you guys need to go back to capitalism school cause you are really bad at it, maybe take example on the US more because 
there's a clear infinite money glitch sitting right in front of you.

Speaking about capitalism, maybe take example on Go Transit, I mean they practically own all of the transport system in 
Canada (not like it's huge but still). I mean their logo is absolutely everywhere, when you tell someone you're going 
somewhere by bus you just feel the need to specify "I'm taking the Go Bus" like they are NOT paying you dude. Everytime 
we'd stop at a station and I'd see the big, greedy logo all I could think about was President Business from the Lego 
Movie and his monopoly on absolutely everything, he genuinely was such a girlboss.

I was also listening to the So True Podcast with Caleb Hearon (it's hilarious btw you should try it) and the guest was 
Trixie Mattel and she was trying to tel Caleb about the song In The Club and he didn't get it so she started singing and 
I love that. I love when people ask you if you know a song and you visibly don't so they just start singing it unprompted 
and there's always that little dance that accompanies the particularly off-key tune and I think that's beautiful. You 
know my philosophy teacher in highschool always said humans don't have instinct because they don't have a reason to live 
as in a biological goal to achieve. But I think that's not true, I think the goal is to make people recognize what song 
you're talking about and the path to that goal is the little dance you make, a mating ritual of sorts if you will.

Thank you very much to all of you for coming to my Ted Talk. I would like to add however that as you are reading me I am
writing from the airport, waiting for my flight and before sitting down and getting comfy for 2 hours because I overplanned 
and got there way too early, I got a matcha latte. Mind you I don't even like matcha lattes, I just thought it's been about 
4 years since I last had one so let me try. So I went to a coffee shop, and like they do at Starbucks, they asked for my 
name so I gave it and then I went to wait for my gentrification infused drink. I finally get it and I kid you not they 
wrote MITCHEL. mind you i SPELLED OUT MY NAME. Honestly that's a hit to my ego because what the fuck do you mean I look 
like my name is Mitchel. I should have just done like I always do and said my name was Sara smh.
