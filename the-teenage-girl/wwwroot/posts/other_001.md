Because we all know it's not as easy as turning 18.

How come one day I get "Don't come home too late, you're only an adult on paper" and the next I leave my father's place 
who hasn't been home in 3 days to go my mom's who's not home either.

I was on my way to vote and I realized that I didn't even feel like an adult then because this has just been my life 
for two years. I'm in no way complaining of course, I love my freedom which I keep repeating to my dad everytime he 
freaks out about being a bad dad.

Yesterday, I signed my hours after working overtime to afford food over the vacation, and now I'm worried about my pay 
coming in and what I'm gonna eat today because the fridge is empty. You see what I mean? I get all the downsides of 
being adult : the worry, the budgeting, the work hours, and none of the benefits. I basically live like all of you so 
why do you keep calling me miss and speaking to me like I just got out of kindergarten?

Let's take a look at a precise case : my cousin. She is now an adult, and ever since she turned 18 I have been telling 
my mother how crazy it feels to have a cousin who is an adult because I saw her basically grow up and I grew up with 
her. And ever since that point she has kept on repeating that that doesn't make her and adult and that she isn't one 
until she acts like one. What is that supposed to mean tho? Because I'm pretty sure I'm acting like an adult, and yet 
she definitely doesn't consider me like one. (maybe it's only cause she's my mom tho) Heck my cousin even owns an 
apartment where she lives with her soldier boyfriend and two cats. She has a job, friends and colleagues, she pays 
taxes, and yet she still gets patronized by the whole family.

Now to be honest, she is a little immature sometimes but then again when you look at her mom you can get why she's not 
exactly straight in the head. And of course my mom and aunts might have a little more experience in the adulting field 
but then shouldn't these expert upper level adults help us? actually give us advice? rather than trying to hold us back 
from growing up to slow down their own aging and then try vainly to evade their impending and inevitable death?

All this to say that yeah maybe I'm not a full-blown adult, but I'm at least an embryo of one and as such, I need a 
little guidance and advice. I'm trying my best to make my way through this very hostile world and I think rather than 
bringing me down y'all should try and help me and my comrades. But then again I guess when I'll be in their place I'll 
act exactly the same way, just as I already do with gen alpha when they say they're grown up.
