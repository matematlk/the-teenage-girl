Things end and those are just new beginnings right? but just how many things change...

The end of the term is showing the tip of its nose (well really the term has been over for 2 weeks but final season is 
still ongoing), and this marks the end of a bunch of things especially a LOT of my friends moving out. That means not 
seeing people for at least 4 months and some of them INDEFINITELY like literally just kill me now.

You know, I thought I was so smart and ahead choosing a uni with a program where I could work at the same time as I was 
learning and make money and all that jazz. Wittle did I know, the big bad monster of depression and sadness was lurking 
at my door, watching me make that decision. So now the consequences of my own actions are coming to bite me in the butt 
because I only know like 7 people with the same sequence as me.

You see, my last exam is on April 24th which means I am moving out on the 25th (I have no one to help me move out and 
into my next residence but that’s another discussion), unfortunately, a lot of the people I know are moving out on the 
17th (which is when my second to last exam is) which means I am going to be basically alone for about a week (kill me). 
So as I have been watching my friends leave one after the other, an impending sense of sadness has been growing into me.

Now a thing I have not brought into light here after the meowzer fiasco of last term (short story I got ghosted), is 
that I have been seeing a guy for a little over 2 months now. And things were going fantastically well until now (except 
for the committed non-committal relationship part) BUT two weeks ago my wife and I went out for our birthday as they are 
3 days apart, and we met up with him and his friends at the club. And we had a phantasmagoric time and I even slept over 
his place and said hi to his mom in the morning but since then he has been a little strange.

A week after that he stayed with me over the weekend, and we went to do errands together and omg. I know he has adhd and 
whatnot but that man was as good as an 8-year-old child. maybe I was already pissed at him, but he was talking about 
girls and every time he does I kinda get irritated even though I’m not supposed to be jealous sigh… And I don’t know it 
just kinda dulled the flame a little and maybe that’s just the rose coloured glasses coming off and maybe even for the 
better but since then he has been less interested in me and doing his own thing, and it’s not even that I feel like a 
background character, I just full on feel like I’m just watching.

On another note I’ve been stressed about final, so I’m trying to shove that down for now but for some reason I’m not as 
stressed as I should be so studying as been soooooo hard like it’s not even funny. Also, my screen addiction has gotten 
so bad that I cannot spend a single second without stimulation. SO I have taken matters into my own hands and turned on 
screentime on my phone so I can limit tiktok time to 3 hours a day WHICH is already something believe me. AND I deleted 
youtube because I got to taking it with me to the shower.

On ANOTHER other note, I GOT A JOB!!! It’s an IT job yes nerd I know, goes with the major I guess. And my trust issues 
have gotten so bad now that I feel like they’re going to fire me before I even start like I actually can’t believe this 
is real. After 6 months of applying and 20+ ghostings I finally got an interview AND got the job. What’s even better, 
the pay is $19/hr AND it’s +0.50$ every term you come back to the job. PLUS if I perform well during the summer I’m 
basically securing a coop job without even having to interview. That is of course if worse comes to worst and I can’t 
find anything else. And the final cherry on top of the wonky, unreliable cake: I have three days of training and they 
are PAID. 8:30 to 4:30 for 3 days and I get MONEY for my trouble. This is what students all around the world have been 
wanting for years.

So yeah to wrap it all up I think I’ve been dissociating for the last two weeks, and it’s all gonna come to bite me in 
the butt when my wife moves out in 2 days. See you then guys (if I’m not dead) (joking)

BUHBYE GUYS!!!
