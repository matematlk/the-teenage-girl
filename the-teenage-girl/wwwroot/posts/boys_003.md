Managing expectations but keeping an open mind

The moment you all have been waiting for... drum roll please… The First Date. Let’s begin from the beginning, the 
3-hour-long, seemingly never-ending and extremely stressful music lecture. So here I am, listening to the teacher talk 
about the Psycho movie soundtrack while I’m slowly breathing less and less thinking about what’s waiting for me after 
the class. Not that I didn’t want it, quite the opposite actually I was waiting for it really impatiently.

Somehow I manage to make it through the lecture before my lungs atrophy and I make my way out to meet him. As I am 
walking outside I see a shy emo boy looking figure in the distance, staring at his phone, so I get closer and deliver 
the fantastical line: “Are you lost baby girl?”. To which I get the response “Are you… ?”. Better than “Get the fuck 
away from me you weirdo” I guess. Anyways, we start walking towards his residence where the private performance was 
taking place, and we get to chatting up.

We get there and I don’t know how to feel about myself because I keep thinking that he doesn’t want anyone he knows to 
see me with him for some reason. We get to his room and all I want is to disappear because I somehow feel like I’m 
intruding even though he literally invited me. And then he picks up his guitar, and he starts playing, and... is that? 
SAY IT AIN'T SO BY WEEZER? GUYS I'M IN LOVE

Ok maybe not in love, but I have this huge grin on my face that I just cannot shake off and I completely forget about 
any stress I might have been in. Before I know it we’re both sitting on his bed next to each other, and he’s teaching 
me the guitar. All of a sudden it’s 12pm, and he asks if I’m hungry (of course I am) so we get up and go get 
cheeseburgers (for which he pays) from burger king. He then brings me to his favorite spot on campus, the roof of a 
cool building where we sit on the ledge eating our burgers and watching the stars. It's so great that I almost forget 
how cold I am until I am reminded by the fact that I cannot feel my fingers anymore.

We then get up and start walking back, and he asks if he can see my room and I say yes because I’m such a gentlelady. 
So we get to my room and of course I feel stressed again because what if he thinks my room is messy, what if it smells 
weird, what if I left dirty clothes on my floor, what if he gets bored with me and he wants to go home, but he doesn’t 
want to say that so he just stays bored out of his mind. But before I know it we’re chatting it up again but this time 
on my bed sat next to each other, and he starts playing with my hair. I go to the bathroom at some point and when I 
come back he’s lying on my bed, so I sit next to him and HE STARTS CARESSING MY BACK, so I take it upon myself to lay 
down ON HIS ARM. heck yeah ladies you can congratulate me thank you, thank you.

All of a sudden we’re cuddling, falling asleep, and he’s putting on a song and says “this makes me think of you”. The 
song was “Wonderful Woman” by The Smiths thank you very much. By that point it’s 4 in the morning and I’m falling asleep
in his arms when suddenly he kisses my forehead and I get chills in my whole body. We keep laying there until he gets 
up to go to the bathroom and when he comes back he says “You have 2 options: you go to sleep, or we go eat right now”. 
Mind you it was 6 in the morning and I had class at 9:30 the next day, so I made the reasonable decision to go to bed 
(I wish I’d gone to eat).

I walk him to the door and he hugs me tight and guys let me just tell you, I still love short kings, but I was 
definitely sleeping on taller men because although I am still a firm believer that 1.90 is too many centimetres for one 
person, 1.78 is just perfect. To conclude, don't overthink what could happen during a date because you'll just end up 
being disappointed that this little detail you might have thought about or seen in a dream didn't go like you thought 
it would and while doing that you won't appreciate what's actually happening. Now mind you I'm telling you that while 
not applying my own advice, so I'll definitely keep working on it.

Anyways kisses and I'll see you later for another episode of my crazy love life.
