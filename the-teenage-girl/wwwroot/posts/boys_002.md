Is it like the movies? Is it just an urban legend?

So... I met this guy. Guess where... you won't: I met him on Hinge... I know, I know dating apps aren't serious, and 
they're unsafe, but they're also so much fun... I mean I can't even begin to explain the amount of dopamine you get 
from just swiping through all these beautiful people, and even better, how good it feels when someone likes you back.

So to start from the beginning. One of my friends got broken up with and to get over it she decided to get the apps and 
I mean ALL the apps. And because I'm such a good friend, I thought I'd accompany her and get one myself. So here we are 
drinking vodka and seltzers in my room on a Tuesday evening and swiping through countless shirtless pictures of men 
holding up fishes.

All of a sudden I see a picture o someone that feels familiar. That's when I remember that really cute grunge looking 
guy from the CS welcome mixer at the beginning of the term that I tried to speak to but didn't quite get to. "OMG LOOK 
I KNOW HIM RAAH HE'S SO CUTE WHAT DO I DO" (that's a direct quote by the way) One problem presented itself, and it was 
that this guy had the same major as me which means if I liked him, and he didn't like me back, and then we bumped into 
each other in a class it would be extremely embarrassing. And that is why I decided not to.

So I go on with my life and about 2 days later I open the app because I'm curious to see who may or may not have liked 
me. Now who do I see but THE CS guy sliding in my dms with the cheesiest pickup line ever (direct way to my heart). 
I answer, of course, and we start speaking. First on the app and then he asks for another form of contact and I give 
him my number.

So we talk for about two weeks, and he mentions that he can play guitar, and he sends me a voice recording of him 
singing the Billie Eilish song from Barbie MEOWING. At that point my heart is fully his and I want nothing more than 
to see this man in real life. After sending me that message he asks me what my favorite song is so he can sing it meow 
version. I tell him *say it ain't so* by Weezer, and he answers "ok say it ain't so meow version recording coming up no 
scratch that LIVE PERFORMANCE". Heart STOPPED. YES PLEASE I think to myself as I answer nonchalantly "sure when tho".

And just like that I had to my calendar "say it ain't so live performance, next Thursday, 10:01pm" (I have a class that 
ends at 10 on Thursdays this is not weird). And I go to bed with butterflies in my stomach, looking forward to that 
Thursday like a coke addict waiting for their next hit. Tune in later for the complete breakdown of the private showcase 
date. :3
