Since the 6th grade when we first learned about the US Constitution and their near and 
dear second amendment, I have been strongly opposed to it. Of course, since I first 
expressed that opinion, I have heard from everyone that it would be impossible to 
remove guns from the US because “It is such a strong part of their culture, you can’t 
simply take them away.” And to this I say: “bullshit”.

Just like you can take a Lego from the hands of a child and let them cry until they 
eventually stop, you can very much take the guns away from Americans and let them 
complain about it for a while. The only thing that will change from their usual 
complaints is they won’t be able to shoot each other to express their discontent 
anymore.

As for the double standard of it all, it suffices to look at the USA’s support of 
Israel. You can’t take back guns because you’ve had them for so long, but you can 
apparently take family’s house, schools, hospitals, and lives because you believe 
this land has always been yours. What an odd thing to do.

Of course, expressing this argument to the US government would be assuming that 
they understand and accept logical arguments, which of course they do not.
